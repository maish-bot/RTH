Who We Are

Started in 2025, grown by word of mouth

Read the Room began as a handful of friends meeting up to talk about the books they couldn't stop thinking about. It's grown into a proper community — new faces every month, one book at a time.

No pressure to have finished it, no judgment if you didn't. Just good conversation, good company, and better book recommendations than any algorithm.
